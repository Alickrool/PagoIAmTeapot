# Romanian household utility bills - knowledge base

> Grounding for the Q&A assistant: the model may ONLY cite causes listed here.
> Checked on 16 Nov 2026. Each fact has a source tag in brackets. "(secondary)" = reported by press/comparison
> sites, consistent across sources, but not read in the official text. Unverifiable claims were removed.

## VAT (TVA)
- The standard VAT rate in Romania went from 19% to 21% on 1 August 2025 (Law 141/2025). [ANAF; EY tax alert]
- Electricity bills for households use the standard rate, 21%. [economisi.ro bill guide (secondary); hotnews.ro]
- A single reduced rate of 11% replaced the old 5% and 9% rates on 1 August 2025. Water supply and sewerage are at 11%
  (9% before 1 August 2025). [ANAF consolidated Tax Code extract]
- VAT applies on top of everything else on the bill (energy, network tariffs, regulated contributions, excise). When
  the energy price rises, the VAT amount rises with it even though the rate is the same.

## Electricity price cap
- The household electricity price cap ended on 30 June 2025 (it was extended until then by GEO 6/2025). Between
  1 April 2022 and 30 June 2025 households paid a capped price of 0.68 to 1.30 lei/kWh depending on consumption.
  Since 1 July 2025 the energy price is whatever the supplier's offer/contract says. [bursa.ro; g4media.ro; GEO 6/2025]
- Vulnerable households can get a 50 lei/month energy aid (GEO 35/2025). [g4media.ro; economedia.ro]
- Suppliers must notify customers at least 30 days before changing the energy price (Law 123/2012, art. 62).
  [economisi.ro (secondary)]

## Gas price
- The fixed household gas cap of 0.31 lei/kWh expired on 31 March 2026.
- From 1 April 2026 to 31 March 2027 a new scheme applies to households: the price on the bill may not exceed
  0.31 lei/kWh. If the contract price is higher, the regulated price applies automatically; customers don't need to do
  anything. [AGERPRES 2 Mar 2026; cursdeguvernare.ro; InfoCons]
- Gas is billed in kWh: the volume measured in m³ is converted using the gas's calorific value.

## Electricity bill components (household, low voltage) - values from 2026
Everything except the energy price is regulated and is the same whatever supplier you choose.
- Energie activă / furnizare (energy): the supplier's price, the only part that changes when you switch supplier.
- Tarif de distribuție JT (low-voltage distribution): paid to the local network operator, set by ANRE per region.
  From 1 January 2026 it is about 0.317 - 0.388 lei/kWh without VAT depending on the region (ANRE orders 75-78/2025).
  In Brașov the operator is Distribuție Energie Electrică România (DEER). [anre.ro order list; economisi.ro (secondary)]
- Tarif de transport (TL): paid to Transelectrica, 0.03645 lei/kWh from 1 January 2026 (ANRE order 74/2025). [economisi.ro (secondary)]
- Servicii de sistem: grid balancing, 0.01272 lei/kWh from 1 October 2026 (ANRE order 60/2026; 0.01470 before). [economisi.ro (secondary)]
- Certificate verzi (green certificates): supports renewable producers. The amount per kWh changes every month with
  the certificate price (about 0.074 lei/kWh in autumn 2026). [economisi.ro (secondary)]
- Contribuție pentru cogenerare de înaltă eficiență: supports efficient combined heat-and-power plants,
  0.01450 lei/kWh from 1 July 2026 (ANRE order 49/2026; 0.01360 before). [economisi.ro (secondary); economedia.ro]
- Contribuție CfD (Contracts for Difference): funds the CfD scheme for new low-carbon power plants. It appeared on
  bills in 2025 and is tiny, 0.000144 lei/kWh from 1 January 2026 (ANRE order 69/2025) - under 1 leu per year for a
  typical home. [profit.ro; capital.ro; economisi.ro (secondary)]
- Acciză (excise): fixed tax per kWh, about 0.0077 lei/kWh for households. [economisi.ro (secondary)]
- Network tariffs and regulated contributions together are a large share of the bill (around 60% according to
  suppliers), so the bill can go up even if the supplier's price doesn't. [economedia.ro]

## Estimated index and regularization
- When the meter is not read, the supplier can bill an estimated consumption (estimat / convenție de consum).
  The customer can send a self-reading (autocitire) in the window shown on the bill or in the supplier's app.
- Estimates are corrected later by a regularization bill. For households, if the regularization shows an overpayment
  above 100 lei, the supplier must refund it within 5 days of the regularization bill; smaller amounts are credited
  on the next bills. [anre.ro - consumer rights]
- Households must give the distribution operator access to the meter so it can be read. [anre.ro - consumer rights]

## Complaints
- First write to the supplier (or the network operator), quoting customer code (cod client), invoice number and
  meter series. Keep a copy.
- If the supplier doesn't solve it, complain to ANRE and attach the supplier's answer, or proof that you wrote to them;
  otherwise the complaint may be closed. ANRE's standard handling time is 30 days. Issues older than 36 months, or
  already in court, are not handled. [ghidullegal.ro; infocontact.ro; ANRE order 61/2026 complaint procedure]

## Switching supplier
- Free for households: no switching fee, and the old supplier cannot refuse. [anre.ro - consumer rights]
- Must be completed within 21 days of the request; ANRE planned the technical switch to take 24 hours from 2026.
  [anre.ro - consumer rights]
- The old supplier must send a final settlement bill (decont final) within 42 days of the switch. [anre.ro - consumer rights]
- ANRE runs an official comparison tool for electricity and gas offers. [anre.ro]

## Telecom bills
- Add-ons (extra TV packages, antivirus, insurance, premium options) are usually monthly subscriptions that renew until
  cancelled; some start free for a promotional period and then become paid automatically. Check the contract for a
  minimum period before cancelling.

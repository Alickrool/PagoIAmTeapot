# Romanian household utility bills - knowledge base

## VAT (TVA)
- The standard VAT rate in Romania increased from 19% to 21% starting 1 August 2025 (Law 141/2025). [VERIFY]
- Electricity and natural gas for households are taxed at the standard rate (21%). [VERIFY]
- VAT is applied on top of everything else on the bill (energy, network tariffs, regulated fees, excise),
  so when the energy price rises, the VAT amount rises with it even if the rate does not change.

## Price caps (plafonare)
- The household electricity price cap (scheme under GEO 27/2022 and its extensions) ended on 30 June 2025.
  From 1 July 2025 electricity bills reflect the supplier's market offer price. [VERIFY]
- The household natural gas price cap was extended to 31 March 2026. [VERIFY - and check what applied after that date]

## What each electricity line means
- Energie activă / furnizare: the price of the energy itself, set by your supplier's offer. This is the only part that
  changes when you switch supplier.
- Tarif de distribuție (JT = joasă tensiune / low voltage): regulated by ANRE, paid to the local distribution operator
  (e.g. Rețele Electrice, Distribuție Energie Electrică România, Delgaz Grid). It depends on where you live, not on your
  supplier. ANRE usually updates distribution tariffs on 1 January. [VERIFY the latest update]
- Tarif de transport (TG/TL): regulated by ANRE, paid to Transelectrica.
- Servicii de sistem: regulated, paid to the transmission system operator for grid balancing.
- Certificate verzi (green certificates): legally required contribution that supports renewable energy producers.
- Contribuție pentru cogenerare de înaltă eficiență: regulated contribution that supports efficient combined
  heat-and-power plants.
- Contribuție CfD (Contracts for Difference): contribution that funds the CfD scheme supporting new low-carbon
  generation. [VERIFY that it appears on household bills and from when]
- Acciză (excise): small excise tax per MWh.

## What each gas line means
- Gas is billed in kWh: the volume in m3 is converted using the calorific value (PCS) of the gas.
- Furnizare: supplier price per kWh (market offer).
- Distribuție: regulated by ANRE, paid to the local gas distributor (e.g. Distrigaz Sud Rețele, Delgaz Grid).

## Estimated index (index estimat / convenție de consum)
- When nobody reads the meter, the supplier can bill an estimated consumption, usually based on past consumption or a
  consumption agreement (convenție).
- The customer can submit a self-reading (autocitire) in the window shown on the bill / in the supplier app.
- Estimated bills are corrected later through a regularization bill (factură de regularizare): if the estimate was too
  high, the difference is credited; if it was too low, the customer pays the difference later in one go.

## Complaints and switching
- First step for any billing problem: a written complaint to the supplier, quoting customer code (cod client),
  bill number and meter series. [VERIFY the legal response deadline under the ANRE performance standard]
- If the supplier does not solve it, the customer can escalate to ANRE (energy) or ANPC (consumer protection).
- Switching electricity or gas supplier is free for households, and the old supplier cannot refuse it.
  [VERIFY the current maximum switching duration]
- ANRE runs an official price comparison tool for electricity and gas offers.

## Telecom bills
- Telecom add-ons (extra TV packages, antivirus, insurance, premium options) are usually monthly subscriptions that
  keep renewing until cancelled; some are free for a promotional period and become paid automatically.
- Cancelling an add-on is usually possible from the provider's app/website or by calling customer service; check the
  contract for minimum periods.
